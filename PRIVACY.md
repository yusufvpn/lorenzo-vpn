# Privacy Policy for Lorenzo VPN

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Lorenzo VPN ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates and safeguards your information.

---

### 1. Zero-Log Policy (Strict Privacy)
- **No Browsing Logs:** We do NOT record, track, store, or monitor your DNS queries, browsing history, destination IPs, or downloaded data.
- **No Connection Logs:** We do NOT log your real IP address, session timestamps, or bandwidth volume linked to any individual identity.

### 2. Information Handled
- **Device & Application State:** The application checks the current version string (`/api/version`) solely to determine if a security update is required to maintain compatibility and protection.
- **Encryption:** All network packets routed through Lorenzo VPN are protected using industry-standard TLS 1.3 cryptographic framing with AES-256-GCM / ChaCha20-Poly1305 ciphers.

### 3. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user personal information with third parties or advertising networks.

### 4. Permissions
- **VPN Service Permission (`android.permission.BIND_VPN_SERVICE`):** Required strictly to route selected network traffic through the encrypted VPN tunnel.
- **Network State:** Required to detect network availability and optimize tunnel stability.
